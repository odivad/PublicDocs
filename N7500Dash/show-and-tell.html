N7500 Dashboard

Full radio control and channel programming for the VGC/Vero VR-N7500 and its Benshi-protocol siblings — straight from your phone over Bluetooth, no cable required.

On-device only, no cloud Built for ham radio operators Free & open
Preparing for closed testing. Every feature on this page is built and verified live on real hardware — this tracks what's actually done, not a roadmap.

These are headless, phone-controlled radios — no keypad, no display, no way to program a channel without an app. N7500 Dashboard is that app: connect over Bluetooth, and every part of the radio's live state and stored channel memory shows up on your phone in real time, editable right there, with a real confirmation step standing between an edit and anything actually sent to the radio.

Built and tested against
Vero VR-N7500
Dashboard

Everything live, one screen

Frequency, mode, signal strength, squelch, TX power, and Work Mode (Single/Dual/Scan) — all synced from the radio in real time, not just read once at connect. A squelch slider sits right on the S-meter: hold for half a second to unlock it, then drag — a deliberate "lock" so it can't move by accident.

  • Slot A/B channel tiles colored to match the VFO display, so what's active and what's standby is obvious at a glance
  • Live TX/RX glow on whichever channel is actually keyed up or receiving
  • Squelch, power level, and work mode all write to the radio the instant you touch them — no separate "save" step, the same as turning a physical knob
Channel Editor

Every field the radio actually has

Frequency, name, repeater offset (with automatic RX calculation), CTCSS/DCS tone, TX power, bandwidth, scan membership, mute, and more — a real numeric keyboard for frequency entry, not a custom keypad. Fields are cross-checked against the vendor's own app so nothing here is guesswork.

  • An app-only "location" note per channel (never sent to the radio) for remembering what a repeater actually is
  • Delete a channel cleanly, or exclude it from the scan cycle, without opening the full editor
Safety

Never writes to your radio by accident

Any edit that rewrites the radio's stored channel memory shows a confirmation step first — always. Long-press a channel for quick actions: Edit, Delete, and two that are deliberately instant (Scan on/off and Mute) because they're single-field toggles a driver needs to flip without stopping to confirm every time.

Groups & Favorites

Save, load, and swap full channel sets

A Group is a saved, named 32-channel set — save what's currently loaded, or start fresh and build one from scratch. Built-in GMRS and MURS templates load the FCC's own band plan in one tap, at the correct power tier for each. Up to 3 Favorites stay pinned in every Group, so the channels you use most are never one load away from disappearing.

Roam

Find the nearest repeaters, wherever you are

Roam pulls the closest repeaters from RepeaterBook based on your phone's actual location and loads them straight into the radio — see the fetch location right above the results, with a one-tap Save that names the new Group after that same place. Auto Roam keeps it fresh: turn it on and it re-fetches on its own once you've actually moved far enough (5, 10, 15, or 20 miles, your choice) to need new repeaters — not on a timer, on distance.
