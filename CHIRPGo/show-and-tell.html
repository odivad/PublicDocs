AT878Sync

A native Android codeplug programmer for the AnyTone AT-878. Read, edit, and write channels, zones, and scan lists over a USB programming cable, with no desktop required.

On-device only, no cloud Built for ham radio operators In active development
Early build, not yet released. Core read/write is working and confirmed on real hardware. This page tracks what's actually done, not a roadmap.

Programming an AnyTone AT-878 today means a Windows-only CPS app and a cable tethered to a desktop. AT878Sync (project name CHIRP-Go) puts that same job on your phone or tablet: plug in the same USB programming cable via OTG, and read or write the radio directly from Android. Need a new repeater? Look it up on RepeaterBook and add it as a channel instead of typing it in.

Connect

Talks to the AT-878 over its own USB cable

Plug the radio in over OTG and AT878Sync finds it, asks for USB permission, and opens a real connection using the radio's own handshake. No vendor driver is needed. One Read pulls channels, zones, scan lists, the talkgroups your channels use, roaming, and radio settings in a single session. One Write puts every change back, after you confirm it.

Channels

Your whole channel list, as a real table

See every channel with the columns you choose: number, name, frequency, analog/DMR, talkgroup, default scan list, and zones. Sort by tapping a header, search by name or frequency, and filter by mode or zone. On a phone, switch to a compact list instead.

  • Change a channel's talkgroup, default scan list, or zones right from the table
  • Tap anywhere else on a row to open the full channel editor
  • Edits stay on the device, marked Unsaved, until you Write them
Talkgroups

Your radio's talkgroups, by name

AT878Sync reads the talkgroup list straight from the radio (thousands of entries) and shows each DMR channel's talkgroup by name instead of a bare index. Picking a different one is a searchable list: type part of a name or number and tap.

The Talkgroups screen is view-only for now. Adding, renaming, and deleting talkgroups is planned.
Repeater Search

Find a repeater, add it as a channel

Search RepeaterBook near your location or a ZIP code or town, within 10, 25, or 50 miles, filtered to analog or DMR and to the bands your radio covers. Tap a result and AT878Sync fills in the frequency, offset, and tone, suggests a name and an empty channel slot, and adds it to your list. Nothing is fetched until you tap Search, and nothing reaches the radio until you Write.

Uses your own free RepeaterBook token, entered once in App Settings. RadioReference isn't available yet.
Zones, Scan Lists & Roaming

The rest of the codeplug, not just channels

Zones and scan lists are read from the radio, shown by name with their channel counts, and written back along with your channels. Roaming channels and roaming zones are read too, with a GPS Roaming Setup card that shows which of the radio's roaming prerequisites are already on.

Radio Settings

General Settings, mapped tab by tab

The AT-878's radio-wide "Optional Setting" dialog, reproduced in the same tabbed layout as the official CPS, with each field's live value read from the radio and CPS-style dropdowns and switches for editing.

Reading is live and working. Writing General Settings back to the radio isn't built yet, so edits here are a preview for now.
Safety

Built so a bad write can't brick your radio

Nothing can be edited until the radio has been read this session, and a red Read radio to edit reminder stays up until it has. Writes never happen without an explicit confirmation. Each chunk's response is verified before the next is sent, and a write stops at the first failure and reports exactly what failed. Unsaved edits survive an app restart, so nothing is silently lost on a dropped connection.

Phone & Tablet

Made for both screens

On a tablet the menu is a pinned icon rail and the channel list becomes a full table. On a phone everything is one tap away in a slide-out menu. It's a native Material 3 app with a dark theme, checked on a real phone and tablet, not just an emulator.

About AT878Sync

AT878Sync requires an AnyTone AT-878 and its USB programming cable. It's not yet published. The AT-878 driver is being built out fully before any other radio is added, and before a public release.
