The AI Teammate

How software teams change when some team members are no longer human.

Buy on Amazon

Most conversations about AI start from the wrong question: will AI replace developers? The better question is how a team works when one of its members recommends, creates and critiques — but has no memory beyond the conversation in front of it and never lives with the consequences of being wrong.

AI behaves more like a teammate than a tool. It contributes. It makes mistakes. It works incredibly fast. It needs context and supervision, and it does better when expectations are clear. That isn't a tool problem — it's a team-management problem. This book is about solving it.

Written from real projects, The AI Teammate covers the roles an AI can fill on a team, the workflow and review loop that keeps it honest, testing that actually catches AI mistakes, and what changes for leadership, managers and developers. It closes with a hands-on setup session your AI runs with your team to build a repository it reads before every session.

AI is not the ground truth. The repository is.
