Privacy Policy — PiStarDash

Last updated: August 24, 2026

PiStarDash is developed by David O'Dell ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the PiStarDash Android app handles data.

Summary

PiStarDash does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with us or with any third party. The app has no backend and no cloud component — it runs entirely on your device.

What the App Does

PiStarDash connects directly, over your local network or your phone's hotspot connection, to a Pi-Star device (an amateur radio digital voice hotspot you own and control) and displays that device's own dashboard data — caller activity, talk groups, mode and network status — in a native app interface.

Data the App Handles

  • Pi-Star admin credentials (username and password for your own Pi-Star device): stored only on your device, encrypted at rest using Android's Keystore-backed encrypted storage. These credentials are sent only to your own Pi-Star device, over your local network connection, to perform the actions you request (e.g. viewing or managing talk groups). They are never sent to us, never logged, and never transmitted to any third party or external server.
  • Network/device information (e.g. Wi-Fi SSID, local network discovery of the Pi-Star device) is used only on-device, to locate and connect to your Pi-Star hotspot. It is not transmitted anywhere else.

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not operate any server that receives data from this app.
  • We do not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, or usage tracking of any kind.
  • We do not share, sell, or transmit any data to third parties.

Data Retention and Deletion

All data (including your saved Pi-Star credential) is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data through Android's app settings, permanently deletes everything the app has stored.

Children's Privacy

PiStarDash is a utility app for amateur radio operators and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect data from anyone, regardless of age.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same location, with a revised "Last updated" date.
